Straight answers.

Why ZAM steel instead of concrete or galvanized steel?

ZAM's aluminum and magnesium form a dense, self‑healing film over cut edges and scratches, where galvanized coatings rust first. In salt‑spray testing, galvanized samples showed red rust by 1,000 hours; ZAM was still clean at 4,000. Unlike concrete, it doesn't crack.

How long does installation take?

About 30 days for a 50 × 25 m competition pool, and 7–10 days for a smaller pool. It needs only flat ground: no civil construction and no welding.

What sizes do you build to?

Competition pools of 50 × 25 × 2 m and training pools of 25 × 20 × 2 m, designed to the FINA (World Aquatics) facilities standard, with water depths of more than 3 m possible.

What is the warranty and service life?

A 15‑year warranty on the pool body and a 50‑year service life.

Can a pool be moved or extended later?

Yes. Because it is bolted, the pool can be disassembled and reassembled without affecting its service life.
